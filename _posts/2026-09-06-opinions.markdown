*There is scarce any Philosopher but dies twice or thrice in Laertius; Nor almost any life without two or three deaths in Plutarch; which makes the tragicall ends of noble persons more favourably resented by compassionate Readers, who finde some relief in the Election of such differences.*  
—Thomas Browne, *Urn Burial*

*It is apparent that there is no death.*  
*But what does that signify?*  
*Not only under ground are the brains of men*  
*Eaten by maggots.*  
—Edna St. Vincent Millay, *Spring*

<br/>

There once was a Thracian named Orpheus—  
The first of philosophy’s laureates—  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;But he hinted that Zeus  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Fancied munching prepuce  
(A prevarication notorious).

## Thales

A relic from the waters of Miletus  
A riddle cast on whom it should devolve  
Which only by the oracle of Phoebus  
Could be authoritatively resolved:  

“All ownership rests fitly with the gods,  
Friends make no petty tallyings of price,  
And virtue the divine affection draws:  
All treasure is the province of the wise.”  

The victor was administered his prize,  
But passed it on again in deference,  
Only to see its ambit circumscribe  
A perfect orbit of presentiment.  

No matter how diffusively they’re strown,  
The perquisites of wisdom know their own.  

## Solon

The legislator’s native Salamis  
Was threatened by Megaran enterprise,  
But Athens lacked a warlike catalyst  
(Dissent precipitated swift demise).  

He garlanded himself in manic wise,  
The reticent agora to lambaste:  
“Give me a city poor and recondite,  
But at these craven infamies aghast!”  

His sanity he certified at last  
And evidenced those words adroitly said  
By falsifying records of the past  
And overturning plots of ancient dead.  

There is no fabricating armistice  
As indefeasible to artifice.  

## Chilon

He posited Olympian auguries  
(Two cauldrons seething with their fires abed)  
Forewarned that children of Hippocrates  
Would bring ruination on their father’s head.  

So Athens deemed itself well-propheted,  
And cursed the old physician’s fruitfulness  
While shouldering its newborn figurehead:  
The triple-tyrannied Pisistratus.  

But valid premonitions recrudesce:  
The seer’s son conquered the Olympic games,  
Whereat his father died of happiness  
(To this the omen truly appertained).  

Distinguishing embrace from stranglehold  
Is oftentimes incumbent on the old.  

## Pittacus

They patronized a barbershop in Cyme;  
His son was sitting underneath the scissors.  
A blacksmith amputated imprecisely,  
Partitioning the boy from gall to gizzard.  

“With ceaseless bow and overflowing quiver  
Will creaturely ferocity be taught;  
A mouth is but a misbegetting fissure  
Whose regnant heart retains malicious thoughts…  

“But lest I be considered overwrought:  
Forgiveness quells revenge with self-control,  
Necessity compels even the gods,  
And as to wealth, the half exceeds the whole.”  

Incapable of castigating malice,  
The logic-chopper’s fatuously callous.  

## Bias

Alyattes laid a siege upon Priene.  
The people dissipated in privation.  
They caterwauled a desperate entreaty  
Unto their shrewdest jurist for salvation.  

He breakfasted two mules past satiation  
And bade them sally forth into the ranks,  
Astonishing each weary battlestation  
With victuals so lavishly disdained.  

He parleyed with commensurate chicane:  
First gathered humble sand in massy heaps,  
Then sowed those fallow mountainsides with grain,  
Securing secretly-propitious peace.  

Omniscience may be only a facade,  
But by such fictions kings are overawed.  

## Cleobulus

The riddle-master’s singular expressions  
Exasperated generations once,  
But only one of his three thousand questions  
Remains in the hereditary fund:  

“A single father sired a dozen sons.  
Each had twice thirty daughters in parade.  
The tenebrous fought others’ scintillance.  
Immortals, they recurrently decayed.”  

One pities those unmarriageable maids,  
For they are neither damsels in their years  
Nor women in experience of days,  
Continually retiring to their biers.  

Why bother to be lucid or concise  
When convoluted metaphors suffice?  

## Periander

He compromised his mother with perversions,  
And though they were performed on her insistence,  
He celebrated (prior to aspersions  
Toward which he was conveniently indignant).  

In barter for some steeplechasing pittance  
He vowed to dedicate a golden idol,  
But overlooked the requisite provisions,  
Descending on the women for their trifles.  

His unmarked grave he trusted to disciples,  
Whose murderers he posthumously murdered,  
And these concatenations homicidal  
Exasperate our most tenacious searchers.  

The sinister’s not basely set aright,  
But censure never minds what’s out of sight.  

## Anacharsis

The Scythian was hopelessly bemused  
That Greeks would dare excoriate in ethics  
The exercise of physical abuse  
While praising pugilistic calisthenics.  

He contemplated their depraved athletics,  
Sardonically divining the solution:  
Anointing oil rendered them splenetic  
Through copious devotional ablutions.  

He ridiculed their sportsmanship’s delusions:  
By bolstering the agonistic crush,  
The thoroughgoingly achieved extrusion  
Of adepts left the amateurs to judge.  

Vulgarities society disclaims  
Are baldly reenacted in its games.  

## Myson

He undertook his mending out of season,  
Devoting summertime to plow-repair.  
Interrogators registered his reason,  
“What more befitting moment to prepare?”  

A man accosted him at unawares,  
Surprised to overhear a boisterous laugh  
Reverberate through isolated air,  
“Elation’s most congenial habitat.”  

An argument is plastic to the facts.  
He designated enquiries conceived  
To vindicate hypotheses inapt  
(Verisimilitude thus supervenes).  

Profundity’s articulate cohesion  
Is secretly assembled by accretion.  

## Epimenides

He dutifully pursued a wayward sheep  
Into the caverned countryside of Knossos  
And wandered in the catacombs of sleep,  
Abiding fifty-seven years unconscious.  

He reappeared amid a world daunted  
By threats of inexplicable sclerosis,  
And undertook the labors of a sophist  
To contradict the dolorous prognosis.  

He shepherded a flock of wooly Ovis  
And loosed it in the Areopagus,  
Presenting lambs as sacrificial votives  
Wherever they succumbed to slothfulness.  

The pupil waits in vain for revelation.  
Discernment comes through grace of hibernation.  

## Pherecydes

Recall the waters’ paramount assessor:  
From distant vantages he could foretell  
If serviceable ships in pleasant weather  
Would perish in an unsuspected swell.  

He once ascribed the flavor of a well  
To seismological catastrophes,  
And like a child eager to retell,  
Earth gave his words a dutiful reprise.  

When moribund, he grudgingly appeased  
Inquirers into his impending death:  
He slid his shriveled finger like a key,  
Attesting through the door his wilted breadth.  

Precociously effusive inundation  
Inevitably foments desiccation.  

## Anaximander

By means of an abstractionist procedure,  
He rescued the Unlimited from mystics:  
Not elemental fire, water, aether,  
Nor any single substance in specific.  

And though its parts are ceaselessly ballistic,  
No trifling fluctuation in the whole  
Distempers its immortal beatifics:  
Profundity’s without restricting shoals.  

His singing was appalling to behold,  
As jeering children’s censures would attest,  
But he forewent the grouses of the old,  
“Without posterity, I’m dispossessed.”  

Cosmology’s apology’s endearing,  
But proof of the rendition’s in the hearing.  

## Anaximenes

He bracketed all limited creation:  
Just menial assemblages of air  
By cunningly minute reticulation  
Producing an illusionary snare.  

His theory made the stellar thoroughfare  
Exclusively obscured by elevation,  
Not split by an abyssal underwhere  
Immune to overland examination.  

Commending heavenly investigation,  
He eulogized that watcher of the skies  
Who, mesmerized by firmament-spectation,  
Once overstepped a precipice and died.  

Imagination, howsoever fleet,  
Is not emancipated from the feet.  

## Anaxagoras

Matter has parts homogenous in kind  
But scrupulously stratified by weight,  
Through principle of motivating Mind  
Upon undifferentiated haze.  

The sun is just a molten iron blaze;  
The clouds deliver thunder by collision  
When winds are reignited by its rays  
(And lightning is emitted by their friction).  

A comet is a planetoid’s ignition,  
The cosmic pole is curiously askew,  
A meteor is air expressing fission,  
And there are habitations on the moon.  

Athens so loved these claims, it was compelled  
To ostracize him from its commonwealth.  

## Archelaus

The world is overhauled by temperature:  
Vapor is water rupturing constraints  
When fire compacts the bulk of it as earth,  
So earth’s by air and air by fire contained.  

Liquids incur interminable pains  
To gnaw the very bedrock where they’re spilt;  
A sea is ocean seeping through the veins  
Such surreptitious tunneling has built.  

From warming pressures under fecund silt  
All nourishing emulsions are derived;  
By oozing through the sublunary milk,  
All life is the inheritance of slime.  

The torque of Fortune’s annular rotation  
Is residue of thermal oscillation.  

## Socrates

Licentiousness is next to demonhood,  
And godliness epistemology.  
He only knew he lacked the only good  
(The pinnacle of anthropology).  

Sculpture belabors stone’s topology,  
Averting thought from virtue’s countenance.  
Surveying land without geometry  
Prefigures counterfeit inheritance.  

“He won’t acknowledge gods in residence,  
Inquiring after altar stones uncouth  
To discombobulate the credulous  
And perpetrate corruption of the youth.”  

Justice is reason overspecified.  
Nature and Athens sentenced him to die.  

## Xenophon

He heard the summons of an emperor  
For voluntary servitude (with honor).  
Afire to be a great adventurer,  
He fabricated Delphi’s imprimatur.  

But Cyrus’ legitimacy tottered.  
Beset with misdirected fratricide,  
Our hero saved his infantry the slaughter  
His kingmaking deficiencies obliged.  

He afterwards incisively described  
The trepid solitude of tyranny,  
Where every intimacy coincides  
With hints of sinister conspiracy.  

The true exoneration of retreat  
Is exquisite attainment in defeat.  

## Aeschines

To more than rivalrous apprenticeship  
Alluded his instructor, Socrates  
With that hereditary genitive,  
“The sausage-maker’s son who honors me.”  

Detractors misconstrued through jealousy  
His faithful emulations of the master  
As counterfeits of lofty pedigree,  
Authentic plagiaristic manufactures.  

He liberated plaintiffs from disaster  
With prodigies of venal prosecution  
Deployed unhesitatingly to flatter  
The weaker argument by elocution.  

Perdition take the blackleg who betrayed  
How statutes and philosophies are made.  

## Aristippus

Who squanders fifty drachmas on a partridge?  
One, notwithstanding charges of conceit,  
Whose taste regards another’s gelt as garbage,  
“So I love birds and you, your currency.”  

At table he would take the highest seat,  
And if the host disqualified that station,  
He’d compliment the magnanimity  
Of granting some new post his elevation.  

Is it philosophy’s humiliation  
To play the mendicant where princes dwell?  
One often sees a doctor visit patients,  
But would not therefore wish to be unwell.  

“Amid the Bacchanalian revelry,  
She who is chaste will not corrupted be.”  

## Interjection: Tritina for Arete

Made indulgently hedonic,  
Insurrectionists ethical  
Pathetically affront constraint.  

Incorrigible ethical  
Susceptibility’s constraint:  
Transfigurations hedonic.  

Recapitulating constraint  
Ineluctably hedonic,  
The epistemic ethical  

Becomes hedonic ethical constraint.  

## Hegesias

The delicate revenge of gratitude,  
Respect’s reciprocal servility,  
Beneficence’s lofty platitudes:  
Mere emanations of utility.  

Decrepitude infests virility,  
The soul is cruelly soldered to the flesh,  
All promise founders in futility,  
Survival cauterizes happiness.  

Abjection, mediocrity, noblesse  
Partake of a continuous domain.  
The favored and the godforsaken wretch  
Are kindred in proclivity to pain.  

The teacher is attentive to his wants,  
But reckons life and death with nonchalance.  

## Anniceris

If ratiocination could surpass  
Habitually inculcated cunning,  
Voluptuousness would be overtasked,  
And confraternity amount to nothing.  

But in the true endeavor of expunging  
The inadvertent evils of our efforts  
No stronger palliation is forthcoming  
Than congregating wretchedly together.  

Where genealogy makes predecessors  
And gerrymandering compatriots,  
Inhabitants nostalgically remember  
The better angels of their sapience.  

Commiserators ineluctably  
Predominate the unaccompanied.  

## Theodorus

The terminations of communal ties  
Are ratcheted around Fortuna’s spool;  
A wise man doesn’t offer up his life  
To extricate incorrigible fools.  

Belief discountenances misconstrual,  
Abhors to deferentially abstain,  
Deciphers dispensations from the rules,  
And cleverly outmeasures the mundane.  

Divulgers of the sacrosanct profane  
Hermetic rites. But how could adepts glean  
Their dogmas, unless deities ordain  
That keepers of the mysteries blaspheme?  

“Don’t intimate I disbelieve the gods,  
Without whose hate you couldn’t be a fraud.”  

## Phaedo

Fastidiously scouring the brothels  
At Socrates’ beseeching, a connivance  
Of followers must purchase an apostle  
Proportioned for receiving such a client.  

The candidate will stay discreetly pliant,  
But demonstrate precocity of vigor  
For plumbing the profundities of science  
With appetite for adamantine rigor.  

He’s old enough to conjugate, configure,  
And condescend to swallow fallacies  
(A feint to disingenuously dicker,  
Then quash his adversary’s calumny).  

The seminal expositor’s transmissions  
Are spent in pedagogic manumission.  

## Euclides

The plenum of ontology is crowded  
By many an inadequate description,  
Its ultimate simplicity enshrouded  
Beneath a clash of nebulous encryptions.  

There is no residence in contradictions  
(Creation won’t accommodate obstruction).  
The good is coextensive with existence,  
Injustices enveloped in destruction.  

When arsenals of logical construction  
Are mobilized to forward the perverse,  
Attack the reprehensible deduction  
And run the syllogisms in reverse.  

If cosmogonic forecasts sanction sinning,  
Then poets manufactured the Beginning.  

## Stilpo

“I’d heard the dweller in the Parthenon  
Proceeded from the vault of Zeus’ temple  
Appareled as befits her pantheon,  
Prefabricated wholly monumental.  

“But what can virtuosity assemble,  
Except the most extraordinary fraud?  
This vacuous chryselephantine vessel,  
Conceived by Phidias, is not a god.”  

His only penance to the demagogues  
(He never said she wasn’t a godess):  
Ejection from the presence of the laws  
(As punishment for looking up her dress).  

Athena wouldn’t sanction an excuse  
For publicly uncovering the truth.  

## Menedemus

The thief who broaches chastity to scavenge  
(Soever cunningly) the greener grass,  
Alert to stick his fingers in some cabbage,  
Emerges with a radish up his ass.  

A hundred things habitually pass,  
Absurdly imitating the eternal,  
Through kindness’ promiscuous crevasse;  
The eagle’s overtaken by the turtle.  

Who’s overcome the cloyingly paternal  
(“Who never beats his father never ceases”)  
Won’t be propitiated by the verbal,  
Nor genuflect to lexical caprices.  

You’ll see the consequents beyond surmise  
Once disputation’s blackened both your eyes.  

## Plato

The myths rechristening Aristocles  
Envision a plateau across his back.  
Contrarily: his broad analyses  
(Conceivably, antipodes attract).  

A paragon progressively refracts  
Kaleidoscopic ambiguity.  
No formulas instantiate intact;  
The number two is less than unity.  

Your soul persists in perpetuity,  
Since opposites proceed from complements.  
In reconciling incongruity,  
The apposite precedes your cognizance.  

Above the dialectic battleground,  
The harmony of verities resounds.   

## Speusippus

The accolades of tributary lackeys  
Commingle under the Academy,  
Incompetent to quench the burning fasces  
Its constituting nepotist bequeathed.  

The secret doctrines of Isocrates  
Cannot amend his fortunate relation’s  
Cascade of petulant monstrosities  
(Perhaps the dog he killed was expiation).  

Each decade of successive arrogations  
Leaves the usurper further paralyzed,  
Too weary to attempt a refutation  
When Cynics advocate his suicide.  

A figurehead is weathered by the gale;  
Antinomies perennially prevail.  

## Xenocrates

A vestment creditably statuesque  
Suffers a harried sparrow to alight;  
No guano-stain’s sufficiently grotesque  
To abrogate the sanctuary-right.  

Nor is the sculpted deity’s despite  
Aroused by insufficient accolades:  
An alabaster mask remits a slight;  
Displeasure suffers splendor to decay.  

The dreamer of immalleable clay  
Awakes within the furniture of wealth.  
His sightless steps are stolidly betrayed;  
He plays iconoclast unto himself.  

Conflating fundament and filigree,  
A monument belies fragility.  

## Polemon

A sybarite suspected to have hidden  
Innumerous apportionments of treasure  
About the city, presaging unbidden  
Contingencies for purchasing his pleasure,  

He graduated to a self-possessor  
By simply being instructed otherwise:  
Enamored of a taciturn professor,  
A profligate untimely proselytized.  

And nothing might induce him otherwise,  
Until his wife’s indictment proved him truant  
In satisfying her domestic rights,  
Maneuvering appointments onto students.  

Let deeds adjudicate if transmutation  
Is genuine, or prestidigitation.  

## Arcesilaus

The student body may affect suspicions  
That entities can’t differ in extent,  
But, once it can appreciate six inches,  
Has trouble with accommodating ten.  

Didactical endowments don’t descend  
On pupils spurning truthful hermeneutics,  
Nor do the fruits of plenitude distend  
The underbrush of internecine eunuchs.  

Judiciously exhibiting her pubis,  
A hen can father clutches on the wind.  
In spite of the dissemblance of the stupid,  
Their gaping mouths let numeracy in.  

Intact isn’t implicitly unbroken;  
Philosophy is knowing when to open.  

## Bion

Deliberate before you raise your hackles  
Where jealousy surpasses expertise.  
Afield, the more intractable the tackle,  
The less it catches of a supple cheese.  

Prepare your cup without precocities,  
And all the bitterness your tongue can bear.  
The more desire wallows in the lees,  
The less becomes compulsory to share.  

The funeral incinerators dare  
To usher us to Hades through the sky.  
Unheeding navigation through the air,  
You find it without opening your eyes.  

If when I die, you want to keep me warm,  
Just set a lantern by my sullen form.  

## Lacydes

Was it the stain of youthful poverty,  
Mementos of subsisting meal to meal,  
Or fresh conceptions of the scholarly  
Which nourished the compulsion to conceal?  

The master’s larders challenge thieves to steal:  
His signet ring lies somehow on the floor,  
Its stamp imprinted on the waxen seal  
That overlaps the hinges of the door.  

But there are servants rummaging the store,  
With neither wonderment nor apprehension.  
They’ve seen the mystery resolved before,  
Wits unintimidated by detection.  

Familiarity breeds unconcern,  
And trust is cultivated, not conferred.  

## Carneades

Appreciate what intellect entails:  
An ardent rector can’t expend attentions  
On cropping hair or manicuring nails,  
Much less respecting sociable attendance.  

Nocturnal lanterns publicized his penchant  
To work beyond whatever fate endowed.  
One night, his eyes were dimmed beyond redemption;  
He took his correspondences aloud.  

At length, the cup of hemlock came around;  
He abdicated Antipater’s lead,  
Attenuating what he first avowed  
To tipple a more amiable mead.  

The sufferer adheres to life from fear;  
What Nature constitutes, she commandeers.  

## Aristotle

Progenitors disseminate conception,  
But who capacitates the youth to think?  
Incomprehension straggles intellection,  
As living flesh eclipses the extinct.  

Our bodies are interiorly linked  
To show their motive force fidelity;  
Souls in camaraderie are indistinct,  
Joint entities’ combined entelechy.  

Ask not which nation rents you tenancy,  
But wherefore tenets merit approbation.  
Reproach a man and not his peccancy,  
Nor yet his essence, but its consummation.  

Extremities of certitude and doubt  
Are medially escaped on walkabout.  

## Theophrastus

Naively auditing is indecisive:  
The University habilitates  
Injunctions with rhetorical disguises,  
Attire the clientele appreciates.  

A censor properly officiates  
Processions of municipal divorces.  
The civic animus asphyxiates,  
Constricted to what policy endorses.  

An ignoramus wantonly discourses,  
But neither can the wise afford discretion.  
A pandemoniac stampede of horses  
Is gallanter than genuine dissension.  

Life’s disappointments overrun successes;  
Unstintingly elucidate your ethics.  

## Lyco

The multitude hallucinates a crown  
Between the corners of the public square.  
Olympus’ empyreal renown  
Condenses where the atmosphere is rare.  

Equestrian instruction seldom spares  
The spur and bridle’s contrapuntal hocket;  
Boys need humiliation to beware  
And covetous competitors for plaudits.  

A maiden’s prospects follow economics:  
Paternal wherewithal to pay her fee.  
The sweetest verses emulate glyconic;  
An apple recapitulates the tree.  

Regretting your own negligence adjourns  
Prospective opportunities to learn.  

## Demetrius

Is there fatality in going blind  
For those reconstituted through Serapis,  
And is a heretic supposed to mind  
If he’s condemned to ruin in his absence?  

Suppose an act of popular chiasmus  
Inaugurates three hundred sixty sculptures,  
And subsequently retrofits their fragments  
As chamberpots and rookeries for vultures:  

The varying perceptions of the vulgar  
Can no more render virtue picayune  
Than practical conceits of civic culture  
Abbreviate the phases of the moon.  

Unknit those eyebrows; though they seem minute,  
They’re your most masochistic attribute.  

## Heraclides

Less cunning than his namesake paragon  
(Confederate to Python’s famed insurgence),  
He merely plagiarized Chamaeleon  
In shades too brazen to escape discernment.  

From childhood he raised a household serpent.  
His pallbearers were nonetheless bewildered  
To raise his squirming funerary curtain  
Above not Lazarus, but his familiar.  

It crowned a lifetime impotence to pilfer  
The nimbus of the gods for honor’s sake.  
He plied the Pythian oracle with silver;  
She died abruptly, bitten by a snake.  

The superficial luster of deceptions  
Illuminates ulterior complexions.  

## Antisthenes

In desperation, only buoyancy  
Commends the suitcases on expeditions;  
The copyist who hearkens loyally  
Becomes the tablet for his own inscriptions.  

The crows might sabotage decomposition,  
But flatterers devour you alive.  
Be patient with an enemy’s conniption;  
You might discover something to despise.  

A general’s authority relies  
On native ancestry to be enforced.  
Enjoying that distinction with the flies,  
He prides himself an ass elected horse.  

My parents weren’t free, nor could they wrestle  
(But none who’ve challenged me have been successful).  

## Diogenes

My father was a banker in Sinope  
(A forgery-obsessed numismatist),  
And there the archaeologists will know me,  
Who marked the minted coins as counterfeits.  

True virtue’s effortlessly underwrit:  
I only need a tub to rest my head,  
One cloak to double up in winter’s grip,  
A staff to fend off buzzards when I’m dead.  

The cure to lust’s much closer than your bed,  
But they don’t like me practicing in public.  
If only hunger were expunged instead  
(By skilled manipulations of the stomach)!  

The lonely road to fortitude accuses:  
There are impediments, but not excuses.  

## Crates

What’re two hundred talents to a pauper  
Unburdened by debaucheries, what worth  
Divides estates above and underwater  
Where vagrant Crates consecrates his birth?  

In Pera, city swaddled in the murk,  
Enriched by soil, forfeiting possessions  
For beggary no parasite perturbs,  
Impregnable to bottom-feeding letches,  

No garlic, thyme, or barleycorn impresses  
Its bearer into quarreling at home,  
Much less abroad with glorious pretensions;  
He barters for the figs and knucklebones.  

The Theban remnant fuels with renovations  
Some future Alexander’s depredations.  

## Metrocles

Disquieting attacks of flatulence  
Deflated his inestimable clout.  
Inordinately pusillanimous,  
He agonized, sequestered in his house.  

His friends’ discreet experiments to rouse  
Morale from metaphysics were ignored.  
They nominated Crates to pronounce  
If he could be cerebrally restored.  

The vagabond erupted through the door  
And sounded forth so thunderous a fart  
That scholar and self-consciousness of scorn  
Were blown irreconcilably apart.  

Condolences are ultimately tactless  
Wherever unexemplified in practice.  

## Hipparchia

Repudiating my prospective suitors,  
I counted their ancestral handicaps  
Endowments intellectually neuter,  
Preferring the ascetic and his scraps.  

You postulate that salutary acts  
By ethics must be fungibly consistent;  
Society’s that solidary pact  
Where each abettor’s everywhere complicit.  

If equities are uniformly licit  
And there’s no harm in maiming volunteers,  
Then ponens tu quoques your ipse dixt:  
You’ve no objection if I box your ears.  

Remember what upended Pentheus;  
Deduction’s the authentic prescience.  

## Zeno

This portico, compelled to play the altar  
When tyrants offered human hecatombs,  
Rededicated to discursive scholars,  
Acknowledges posterity anew.  

The aristocracy of rectitude  
Is inoffensively infallible,  
Embracing what antagonists impugn  
As idiomatically impractical.  

An aphorism isn’t fashionable  
By virtue of its apodictic merit,  
No more than Alexander’s mandible  
Endows our coins with ancillary karats.  

The sages aren’t immutably austere;  
You only apprehend them by the ears.  

## Ariston

The rhetorician’s pious masquerade  
Reveals what’s pedagogically most timely:  
At turns, he galvanizes and dissuades,  
Portraying Agamemnon or Thersites.  

Whoever barred the skeptic from forthrightly  
Admitting testimony of his conscience  
Concerning the quotidian benignly  
Allows him to assimilate the monstrous.  

The victim of a formalist demonstrance,  
Engrossed with intertwining warp and weft,  
Belatedly discerns the whole’s preposterous  
And forfeits disentangling the web.  

A notion not immediately useful  
Can only signify with your approval.  

## Herillus

A statue’s singularity’s beyond  
The curvature of sculpturer and sitter;  
Outlasting the illustrious, its bronze  
Successively incarnates, reconfigured.  

A child might precociously consider  
Virility entangled in his tresses,  
But delegates morality to scissors  
(Or lovers of protracted adolescence).  

Require an exhaustive convalescence  
To rehabilitate the unreflective.  
Preoccupied with accidents of presence,  
They deviate from ultimate objectives.  

Cognition is a pattern of reception  
Compulsively indignant at correction.  

## Dionysius

Who boasts of holding anguish in suspension  
Misunderstands its posthumous indifference,  
Securely retrojecting pain’s declension  
Through memory’s vicarious deliverance.  

Impelled with inescapable insistence,  
You’ve audited atrocity unblent,  
Implacable through intimate perditions—  
Nor ever overcame, but underwent.  

The cataleptic’s fantasies relent;  
Intoxicated with excruciation,  
The ulcerated eyes of discontent  
Impenitently execrate creation.  

If misremembering’s hypocrisy,  
Theodicy demands apostasy.  

## Cleanthes

A dissonance of exercise and motives  
Is only incidentally malign.  
An ass at least accommodates its onus;  
The craven stays inculpably benign.  

Whereby Aristoteleans, who opine  
On moral equilibrium, are lyres,  
Emitting their mellifluous designs  
Without the need to audit what transpires.  

The fewer superfluities required,  
The narrower proximity to health,  
But when the last periphery’s expired,  
A pragmatist dispenses with himself.  

The water-drawer modestly persists  
In pantomime of emptying the Styx.  

## Sphaerus

Acknowledging the pomegranate’s ripe,  
Though craftsmanship can conjure artifacts  
More appetizing than their archetype  
Encapsulating only candlewax  

(Against the falsifier who attacks  
An underfed disciple’s erudition  
For crediting the fabricated facts  
And carelessly acquiring opinions),  

Is not neglecting tentative decisions,  
But, utilizing quantitative ledgers,  
Determining from testimony given  
The rational, if unestablished measure.  

In keeping categorically disjoint,  
We never quite capitulate the point.  

## Chrysippus

If you articulate what you imagine,  
It passes through your mouth beyond the lungs,  
So if you’ve ever verbalized “a wagon,”  
You’ve made a wagon roll across your tongue.  

When there are several heads to choose among,  
Not all of them can underpin your face,  
And “heads-you-haven’t” tallies over one;  
Numerically, your cranium’s erased.  

You carry what you haven’t yet misplaced,  
So never having lost a pair of horns,  
(In spite of your evaporated pate)  
You forehead is implicitly adorned.  

His holy genealogies were raunchy,  
And he expired laughing at a donkey.  

## Pythagoras

Waylaid by rascals torturing a whelp,  
He ordered the attackers to abstain,  
To recognize those pitiable yelps  
As from a friend unworthy of his pain.  

For every seed conceals an infant brain  
Invisibly desiring control,  
To blossom with a latticework of veins  
For capturing its unsuspecting soul,  

And Aethalides carries every role  
In memories across eternity:  
Sees Hesiod atop the brazen pole  
And Homer hanging from the serpent-tree.  

Don’t agitate the fires with your knife.  
Don’t piss into the consecrated light.  

## Empedocles

The tinkling of his footsteps is portentous;  
The heavens near climactic turnabout:  
Aridity miraculously quenches,  
Torrential rains capitulate to drought,  

An unrelenting windstorm turns to rout,  
Sporadic wisps of calm are recollected,  
Fecundity prevails, the fallows sprout,  
The somnolescent dead are resurrected.  

But lately, he maneuvers undetected,  
(Though fables of his whereabouts are rife),  
Unless departure signals he’s ascended,  
Occulted from the churn of death and life.  

From Etna’s fiery depths, a lava-chute  
Disgorges bronze remainders of a boot.  

## Heraclitus

Becoming vitiates hypostasis.  
The whorl and irradiance of fire  
Transpose in asymmetric consonance;  
The soul is an unfathomable pyre.  

The subsequent elaborates the prior;  
Calamities facilitate creation.  
Enlightenment foreshadows what transpires;  
Serenity arouses conflagration.  

Tumescent with the fruits of abnegation,  
The visionary wallows in manure;  
Immobilized by thorough incubation,  
His carcass is imperishably cured.  

Fate’s realigned but undelayed by fasting;  
Impermanence alone is everlasting.  

## Xenophanes

Although the elements are only four,  
Our planet has innumerable peers:  
Unique assemblages lie unexplored,  
Immutable through cumulative years.  

The godhead is proportioned like a sphere,  
All vantages to equitably see,  
Contentions to disinterestedly hear,  
Not countenancing apertures to breathe.  

Our hexametric poetry deceives,  
Enveloping the gods in human skin;  
The caricature-peddler believes  
What insulates himself from what’s within.  

The wise men of the Earth are undiscovered  
Because they'd need to recognize each other.  

## Parmenides

Our Being issues from a single locus  
Extending equally in all directions.  
Combined ubiquity and pluripotence,  
It’s unexceptionally comprehensive.  

Subsistence is disordered by the senses,  
Though echoing the cogency of truth;  
Ignore the indefensible contentions,  
But steadily interrogate the proofs.  

As intimate as instrument and use  
Are architected earth and forging flames;  
The mind and soul are welded at the roots,  
So Lucifer and Vesper are the same.  

Realities are by necessity;  
Whatever’s nonexistent cannot be.  

## Zeno

However lackadaisical, a tortoise  
Can overmatch Achilles’ swiftest pace,  
Provided he’s permitted but the shortest  
Of handicaps on entering the race.  

By capturing the turtle’s former space,  
The runner, gaining relative position,  
Approximates but hardly overtakes,  
Nor does his place improve with repetitions.  

Beleaguered by invisible sedition,  
Nearchus grants an audience to hear  
A whispering conspirator’s admissions:  
The rogue philosopher bites off his ear.  

Be vigilant for multiplicity,  
Reluctant to approach infinity.  

## Leucippus

The Limited is a receptive void  
Suffused with flotsam from the Infinite  
Profusely intermingled and conjoined,  
Then decomposed into constituents.  

But some form microcosmic firmaments  
To passively accumulate detritus,  
Progressively decanted distillates  
Evaporation quickly crystallizes.  

The nucleus concentrically arises,  
Eroding its entropic reservoir  
Until an errant flicker catalyzes  
The advent of an opalescent star.  

Conglomerates and individuals  
Develop by consistent principles.  

## Democritus

Causation recognizes precedence,  
Not prodigies of contiguity;  
An antecedent is necessitous,  
Precluding existential truancy.  

Atomic solids dot Vacuity  
With pointillistic bodies of convention,  
Belying their discrete impunity  
From turbulence of structure and dimension.  

But even an intangible reflection  
Arises from particulate bombardment:  
The soul and its attendant apperception  
Are fantasies made corporately incarnate.  

Wherever superstitions are eschewed,  
The spirit realizes quietude.  

## Protagoras

An answer fosters its antithesis,  
But humans always arbitrate the questions,  
Give authorized conceits their immanence  
And obsolete ideas supersession.  

Agreement, not conducive to possession,  
Precipitates from worthy conversations  
Where casuistry teases circumspection  
And apodixis grapples its negation.  

The master will defer his compensation  
Until each student triumphs in the the courts,  
So anyone forestalling litigation  
Can be accused of self-fulfilling torts.  

There aren’t ontological partitions  
Between discoveries and disquisition.  

## Pyrrho

When buffeted by inclement caprice,  
The pig alone exhibits perseverance;  
Through human pandemonium, it eats,  
Untroubled by unwarranted adherence.  

We can’t sincerely gainsay an appearance,  
Or live without eliciting traditions,  
But secrets of constitutive inherence  
Inspire no endogenous conviction.  

(And least of all the prior propositions:)  
Raise each conjecture to its apogee;  
Credentials in coequal opposition  
Engender a benignant apathy.  

The dogmatist and doubter feign submission  
Where skeptics recommence the inquisition.  

## Epicurus

Sensations can’t discredit one another:  
Within a single mode there’s no preemption,  
While different faculties’ domains are sundered,  
And reason only explicates the tension.  

Acknowledgment requires preconception,  
So all experience is factual:  
Nonentity produces no impressions;  
A madman’s reverie is actual.  

Research alleviates imaginal  
Enigmas clouding death and genesis,  
Depriving antiquated magical  
Equivocations of their resonance.  

Desires anodyne when unfulfilled  
Are only efficacious uninstilled.
