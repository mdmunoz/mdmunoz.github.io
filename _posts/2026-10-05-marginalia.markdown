title:  "Marginalia to Phenomenology of Perception"

*We are like fishes swimming in the sea of sense, bounded above by the superior element, but unable to breathe it pure or penetrate it.*  
—William James, *Pragmatism*  
  
The many-angled houses of my sight  
are traced from an elusive diagram  
beyond compulsion, the intangible.  
And pieces of my eye are alien  
to marginal, entreating countrysides  
eclipsed by focus. New horizons crease  
the bounding ripples of my plunging stride,  
entrusting their surroundings to suspense:  
perspectives radiate integrity.  
This cosmos of elective habitats—  
exposed from every avenue in space  
by mirror-facet furniture—enjoins  
the recognition of assembled time.  
Self-evident to early innocence,  
inevitable through experience,  
the house subsists, a vein of future past.  
I pluck it from the course of evidence,  
contriving an abrupt eternity.  
The obverse of my action supplements  
descriptions of the obvious. The past  
itself is altered by my emphases.  
Coherence is a limit of decay  
accumulating in conspiracy:  
exploded infrastructure’s reticence  
to entertain my narrowing surmise.  
But persevering is an ecstasy,  
performative amnesia a cascade  
of interposing objects in the time  
of uniform extension. Here my eyes  
are equally material, aligned  
by fixed geometry to everything.  
The *universe* articulates my *world*  
and inner touch retreats from common sense.  
A crystal logic tessellates throughout  
the unsuspecting furrows of my brain.  
  
A mechanism’s receptivity  
encourages revisions to the web  
of circular relations. But a sense-  
capacity belies analogies  
to organizing patterns of the mind.  
Specific structures’ atrophied response  
desaturates, instead of puncturing,  
the total apprehension. Color-schemes  
diffuse into a slow dichotomy  
of blue and yellow. Stimulations keep  
their secret conversation from the reach  
of ganglia. Imaginary signs  
meander through identities of touch.  
Resemblance is procedural, a route.  
My hands anticipate entanglement  
with imitation. I enact myself.  
The recognition blurred along my limbs  
might haunt me into amputation’s long  
hiatus of exterior complaints.  
An insect compensates for absent legs  
by answering the same conundrum posed  
when walking as before, an interplay  
organic logic summons from itself.  
It rides melodic spontaneity.  
Somatic reflex is contextual,  
enchanting situations with conceit.  
An inner resonance prefigures sight.  
I’ll lose the world before I lose my eyes  
(or vice versa), echoing alone.  
My entourage of limbs is registered  
unconsciously. I’d often be surprised  
if tacit use were were disappointed by  
an absence or addition in the act.  
Embodiment imbues affordances.  
Capacities of use revolve around  
the competence implicit in my hand  
(although it may be severed from the wrist).  
My body wears a habit of itself.  
Time can’t erode an unaccomplished task  
or rectify an existential scar:  
the expectation of an answer yawns  
beneath the surface-tension novelty  
of memories in sorted parallax.  
I skim the sprawling edifice of time,  
an unknown knot of trivialities.  
My nervous system’s automatic script  
defaults me onto further avenues,  
however I envelop it with grief.  
Reflexes carry personality,  
like heartbeats sounding tributary veins.  
The past’s enameled dogmatism bleeds  
provisional coherence from the *now*.  
Emotion seals ambivalent events’  
unmanageable connectivity  
(which only banishment can rectify).  
My senses run through tandem circuitry  
with data that observe their etiquette,  
absolving me from animal concern,  
suggesting exit through self-evidence.  
The faces on an autonomic clock  
wear theater masks throughout their histories.  
I think, therefore… (and if there’s someone else?)  
I answer to my physiology.  
  
My bodyparts are inescapable  
by virtue of partitions they impose:  
decapitations of ontology  
that never ricochet into themselves  
(a mirror doesn’t count, apparently).  
The touched and touching organs are distinct—  
insensate gristle and its poltergeist.  
I don’t rehearse a chain of custody  
(in proximate extremities) for pain.  
*My foot hurts*. Seeds of movement propagate  
intentions through my arm’s trajectory.  
  
My hands take up schematic space—conjoint,  
transposable coordinates that end  
the world’s mosaic singularity.  
Dynamically-composited for use,  
my self-locations taper from the poles  
I conjure up, like comet-trails through space.  
I’m always somewhere in the audience:  
a set piece, background, and the theater-dark.  
My prepositions tell their origins  
in tacit sense and oriented sight.  
Familiar gestures are concrete in scope,  
accomplished without access to the stage  
of abstract imitations and surprise.  
Intentions thread my fingers through the tools  
and pull them taskward, like the gravity  
of social nicety on attitude  
(I *am* whatever others think of me).  
I don’t think twice when, beckoning my friends,  
impatience wanes and waxes in my hands.  
The distance is conveyed without design  
until there’s no one to accost, and arms  
revert to mechanisms of intent.  
I conjure leylines and symbolic guides  
onto my fixed surroundings and pretend  
interior concerns into the world.  
Discovery implies intention: facts  
don’t carry explanations for themselves.  
Touch isn’t adequate for abstract space.  
A shifting catalogue of motion-points  
lacks speculative continuity.  
The systems of behavior, interlaced,  
applied with equal probability,  
render induction undecidable.  
One atrophied capacity belies  
the others’ understanding of themselves:  
their purity is pathological.  
Without the pointing finger’s *axioms*,  
empiricism is impossible.  
Objectifying power is consciousness  
simpliciter, intentions interlaced.  
Even my reflexes are presupposed.  
Mistakes, derangements, seasonal complaints  
are Being penetrating me at will,  
despite the symbols I construct within  
(or else all madness is malingering).  
Acausal founder of significance,  
my vision, nature’s Spirit-gift, is ground  
embodying me, existential grist.  
Even the sublimation into form  
depends on concrete spontaneity.  
Reason consumes a litany of facts  
for every simile it consecrates:  
fur:cat::feathers:bird.  
Authentic subjects synthesize the *now*;  
syncretic phantoms wait outside of time.  
The sediments of conversation tinge  
a friendly word. Directions fill my limbs.  
An object speaks intention’s spawn in waves.  
A sentence shuffles world-scenery  
(unless imagination stacks the deck).  
Even geometry assumes a field  
of plastic illustrations, idioms  
to prove its isometric arguments.  
I wander landscape-physiognomies  
that speak me to myself and house my thoughts.  
They mark the stresses, turning happenstance  
to narrative by hidden prosody.  
I tell my numbers as a ritual,  
without resorting to arithmetic.  
A situation’s rainbow unifies  
sensation with its ideology.  
My consciousness is not a catalogue  
(it suffers chance involuntarily),  
nor is its function categorical  
(it undergoes exceptions and decay):  
dissociation billows from its core.  
I’m not *inside*, but I *inhabit* space.  
  
My total motion self-assembles arcs  
indifferently among alternatives,  
but I can think my innards into view.  
My constitution can amend itself.  
The body is a composition, flecks  
of time and gesture offering a life  
inseparable from its artisan.  
A tool, familiarized through habit, draws  
a farther boundary along the skin:  
perception by acquisitive degrees.  
  
Primordial reality exists,  
subsists without my intervention, but  
affective incarnation swells. They say:  
of pain and pleasure, nothing can be said.  
I’m one part undisclosed homunculus  
with outsized genitals and gendered smile,  
whose static leer interrogates events  
for satisfaction from unconscious planes—  
one body to another—constantly.  
Desire guarantees my history  
(which any impetus can commandeer,  
confusing coupled incapacities).  
I might lose speech without hypocrisy,  
like memories in an amnesiac,  
or flee intolerable circumstance  
for apathetic world-totality.  
By imitating slumber, I perform  
hypnosis on myself, invoke the god.  
The cure for sleep is passive vigilance;  
hysteria, complete epiphany.  
My nudity’s an ultimatum cast  
against observers. Open to the glare  
of alien intelligence, I *must*  
submit myself in abject modesty  
or dominate with animal disdain.  
But glamouring him into impotence,  
humiliation and captivity  
inspires contempt: he doesn’t signify.  
Ontology is sexual. I lose  
my body’s given structure when aroused.  
I never overcome the atmosphere  
diffusing like an odor from my loins  
and ferrying the metaphors of dream.  
A serpent is my penis typified  
(existence has erotic silhouettes).  
Freedom and instinct, even unto God,  
are *actually indeterminate*.  
Mankind is not a species, but a thought.  
And sexless men are inconceivable.  
  
My language is a late arrival, but  
its origins were overheard as facts.  
No one was speaking those primeval words.  
And when I talk with spontaneity,  
my mouth emits conditioned sentences.  
But words are more than envelopes for thought.  
Sense-bearing speech is recognition. God  
creates by name (apart from animals).  
If knowing’s more than fabulation, less  
than fragments of an ancient memory,  
then words engender meaning, gesturing  
from someone’s understanding to my own.  
I hear philosophy’s aesthetic mode,  
the consonance of thought within its speech.  
The orator and auditor are linked,  
allowing rhetoric to overrun  
their margins of reflection and assent.  
My words are always ready—at my back  
until I sound them out (and into place).  
Expression infiltrates me from the heart,  
embeds its signifying parasite  
with organs sensitive to argument’s  
ethereal dimensionality.  
The signifier overtakes its sign,  
an actor is abducted from the stage  
by discontinuous realities.  
Each quiet recollection dignifies  
a seething underworld of dialect.  
Cacophony pervades my inner life,  
diffusing into novel silences.  
I catch lascivious intent in smiles  
without an inventory of desire.  
I’m puppeted by anyone’s caress.  
(But only where the gestures presuppose  
my own capacities and attitude:  
a dog’s seduction is a simile;  
an insect’s pheromones are semaphore.)  
And words are also gestures, sing the world,  
conveying nothing other than themselves.  
There is no human nature. Every mood  
shares institution’s ambiguity.  
A name too-long repeated turns unreal.  
My actions are transfigured by degrees  
beyond themselves: behavior, gesture, speech.  
Air, hissing past the throat to tongue and teeth,  
transcends its literal embodiment  
like love emerging from within desire.  
My body narrows into thought, with all  
confusions and dichotomies intact.  
  
The world besieges subjectivity,  
a shipwreck rooted in the crashing waves.  
Blue coaxes me to understand its shape.  
I slow my breathing to the pace of sleep,  
align my mouth with an external lung,  
communing with the spirit of the air.  
The sky extends reciprocating light:  
blue, fathomlessly, where it thinks itself.  
I am the sky.
